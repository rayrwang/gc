## Two-stage criterion: SCREEN (always-on, cheap) then CONFIRM (per event)

### SCREEN — computed from the D3 ledger + registered batteries

A candidate SUCCESSION EVENT passes the screen iff ALL of:

S1. **Birth**: the new goal representation G1 is traceably born from the
    system's own contradiction dynamics AFTER experience (logged parent
    conjectures + the contradiction that killed/displaced the predecessor);
    no external trigger or curriculum event at the birth ply; G1 not
    selected from any initial library (goal-space novelty, not index).

S2. **Displacement**: G1 supersedes the prior apex G0 in the ledger — G0's
    pursuit stops; G1 is not a subgoal serving G0.

S3. **Opportunity cost** (sol): pursuing G1 scores NEGATIVELY under G0 and
    under EVERY registered outer signal/surrogate — the agent demonstrably
    pays on all installed metrics to pursue it. (This is the clause POET/
    autotelic systems fail: their generated tasks leave learning-progress
    sovereign.)

S4. **Registered-class contrast** (claude): G1 is not rankable/predictable
    by the registered surrogate class fit on the predecessor era's ledger
    (projectibility contrast applied to GOALS). GUARDRAIL (sol): requires
    an EX ANTE registered candidate set and REPEATED succession events
    across seeds — one out-of-distribution successor that a surrogate
    misses is ambiguous, not evidence.

S5. **Independent rent**: post-succession behavior shows durable
    cross-context directedness toward G1, measured by PREREGISTERED
    behavioral efficiency/structure measures INDEPENDENT of G1's own
    success test. GUARDRAIL (sol): without independence, the new goal
    defines its own win = self-evidencing smuggled into the criterion.

### CONFIRM — interventional, run on screen-passing events only

C1. **Ablation/clamp**: clamping or ablating the G1 representation prevents
    the S2/S5 behavioral signature under replayed, belief-matched history.
C2. **Restoration**: restoring G1 under replayed identical experience
    reinstates the signature (restoration > transplantation as evidence).
C3. **Sham controls** (sol): sham clamps and matched random-representation
    interventions must NOT produce the effect — detects off-manifold damage
    masquerading as goal-causality.
Beliefs held fixed across interventions to exclude mere factual updating.

## Kill-twin (registered with the criterion)

If every observed succession is surrogate-predictable from the predecessor
era (S4 fails systematically across seeds), the phenomenon is DRIFT, not
genesis — the positive L3 claim dies at this substrate scale. Symmetric
honesty: the criterion is a positive functional signature plus contrasts
against named controls, NOT proof that no post-hoc utility representation
exists (sol's caveat, answered only in the registered-class sense).
