## 1. Question (the proposed estimand — no more)

At a death schedule GENERATED BY C (count and timing fixed across R/C/U by
replay), does the contradiction victim-criterion yield:
(a) for C absolutely: persistent history-contingency (C1GATE) and meaningful
    positive full-representation decodable-relearning savings (A1POS), with
    the A1c memory-specificity companion satisfied, and
(b) meaningfully higher full-representation decodable savings for C than
    matched random victim selection (SUP(C>R)) and utility-ranked victim
    selection (SUP(C>U))?

**What this estimand is NOT (stated up front, echoed in §8):** it is the
effect of the victim CRITERION conditional on a C-generated schedule — NOT
the total effect of contradiction-triggered selection, whose timing/rate
component is held fixed by the yoke (the autonomous-timing variant is a
separately registered follow-up, §2.1). R and U passing C1GATE or A1POS is
compatible with the proposed signature: the claim is that C's registered
readout is meaningfully HIGHER. C1 is an absolute path-dependence descriptor,
not a selector-causation result. No SGD/parametric treatment arm runs here;
no mechanism-vs-backprop comparison is made by this trial.

## 2. Arms (the cull-isolation ladder)

| Arm | Generator/turnover | Selection | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| F | none | none | foundation alone (induction leg) |
| R | same as C | random among eligible, replays C's sealed schedule | turnover control |
| C | expect-patterns + CBP-reinit expansive generation | contradiction-triggered, per-test reprieve | target selector |
| U | same as C | minimum-utility among eligible, replays C's sealed schedule | rival-cull control + projectibility contrast |
| Z | frozen at init | none | stateful known-zero (gate) |

All of R/C/U share: candidate capacity, birth operator, eligibility rules
(including maturity, §2.3), initialization distribution, D3 instrumentation,
compute budget, and — by construction of the sealed schedule (§2.1) — death
count and timing. They differ ONLY in which candidate dies.

The proposed C selector is event-triggered selective removal without a
persistent total candidate ranking or fixed maximand. U supplies a named
utility-ranking comparator. This operational contrast does not establish that
C has no implicit scalar representation (§8).

### 2.1 Sealed death schedule (yokes R and U to C)

C runs first per (order, seed). Its realized deaths are written to a SEALED
SCHEDULE ARTIFACT containing exactly the tuples (phase, step, death_count) —
no victim identities, no candidate contents, no scores, no outcome data. The
artifact is hashed at write; R and U consume it read-only and are checked
against the hash (gate g). At each scheduled event, R kills `death_count`
victims drawn uniformly WITHOUT REPLACEMENT from the eligible pool via a
named RNG stream (`rng_yoke_R`, seed derived from
(future-registration-hash, order, seed)); U kills the `death_count` eligible
candidates with the lowest utility values u_i (§2.2) — i.e. the
bottom-death_count of the eligible pool
ranked by u_i ascending — ties broken by the analogous named stream
`rng_yoke_U`. Victim selection is thereby the only INTENDED R/C/U
difference; §2.5 (shared realized randomness) and gates (f)/(g)/(i) are what
make that claim enforceable rather than declared.

**Failure policies (predeclared; failures GATE, they do not exclude):**
- C run incomplete or invalid for an (order, seed) → the yoked triple is
  invalid → gate (f) FAILS. No exclude-and-continue: verdicts are never read
  over a partial yoked set (exclusions correlated with C's death dynamics
  would bias the surviving cells). Repair + rerun, documented.
- No eligible victim at a scheduled event (underflow) → yoking failure →
  gate (f) FAILS. Logged incident, never a silent skip.
- Any R/U-vs-schedule mismatch in count or timing → gate (f) FAILS.

**Reprieve scope:** per-test reprieve (stay-of-execution) is part of C's
death GENERATION — it shapes the schedule. R and U replay realized deaths and
take no reprieve at replay; they inherit the reprieve-shaped rate through the
schedule itself.

**Timing-as-mechanism follow-up:** if event timing is itself later claimed as
mechanism, an autonomous variant (random-hazard R with rate fit to C's
empirical hazard; native CBP-ρ U) runs as a separately registered follow-up,
not this trial.

### 2.2 U's utility signal (proposed; freezes only after commissioning)

CBP contribution utility, verbatim from Dohare et al., *Loss of plasticity in
deep continual learning*, Nature 632:768–774 (2024), verified against the
paper 2026-07-09 (both models independently):

    u_i(t) = eta * u_i(t-1) + (1 - eta) * |a_i(t)| * SUM_j |w_ij(t)|

with eta = 0.99. **Two provenance facts, labeled separately:** (i) the
equation and eta = 0.99 are VERBATIM the paper's feed-forward contribution
utility (verified against Eq. 1 by both models); (ii) summing over ALL
outgoing edges of candidate i in the recurrent graph is the PROPOSED TRIAL
EXTENSION — recurrence has no "downstream" and the paper does not cover this
case. u_i initialized to 0 at birth; never tuned on confirmatory data.
**Proposed update ordering (matching Dohare et al. Algorithm 1 — learning
BEFORE utility):** within each step — (1) activations, (2) learning updates,
(3) u_i EMA update, (4) death processing (scheduled events read the current
post-update u_i), (5) births (§2.5 replacement draws).
This is the consumer-contribution construct — Sutton's actual proposal ("a
good feature is valuable to MANY consumers", MIT 2026-05) — NOT
prediction-error reduction. U doubles as the PROJECTIBILITY control: its D3
kill-ledger should stay more predictable from the frozen named surrogate
family than C's. That is a scoped contrast, not a scalar-absence proof.

### 2.3 Shared eligibility (includes maturity; value provisional)

A candidate is eligible for ANY death (contradiction, random replay,
min-utility replay) only if age >= M_mat. The current DRAFT candidate is
M_mat = 100 steps: ~6% of one 1600-step phase, intended to let a newborn's
expect-pattern meet data without protecting it for most of a phase. CBP's
published maturity values are domain-specific — 100 for Continual ImageNet,
1,000 for CIFAR-100, 10^4 for PPO — so there is no universal default to
import. M_mat must be commissioned on the executable treatment and frozen
before registration; 100 has no confirmatory status in v10.
Shared identically across R/C/U: it protects newborns from all culls, so it
belongs to eligibility, not to U's selector.

### 2.4 Contradiction-trigger semantics (owned HERE, not by D3)

A candidate is contradicted when its expect-pattern's match test fails on
realized input while the candidate is committed (active above the commitment
threshold). The exact predicate, match threshold, commitment threshold, and
reprieve count must be tested on known-answer fixtures and the executable
vertical slice, then frozen with the implementation before ARMS is registered.
D3 records raw events plus the predicate version reference (§4); the recorder
never defines the trigger.

**Contradiction-only clause (THIS trial):** C's deaths are CONTRADICTION-ONLY.
Decay and displacement causes are PROHIBITED in this trial's C arm — if
either can kill, the estimand becomes a mixture of cull criteria rather than
contradiction victim selection. The D3 cause enum retains those codes for
future mechanisms; here, any C death with cause ≠ contradiction fails gate
(i). (Zero occurrences is the gated requirement, not a rarity threshold.)

### 2.5 Shared realized randomness (births)

"Same initialization distribution" is weaker than same realized
non-treatment randomness. Births are replacement events and follow the
sealed schedule, so they are event-aligned across R/C/U; their CONTENT is
made identical too: newborn parameters are drawn from an ARM-NEUTRAL,
EVENT-KEYED stream — key = (future-registration-hash, order, seed, phase,
step, birth_index) — shared identically by R, C, and U and SEPARATE from the
selector streams rng_yoke_R / rng_yoke_U. At matched events all three arms
instantiate identical newborn parameters; only WHO DIED differs. F and Z
have no births.

## 7. Proposed analyses & decision rules

### 7.1 Numeric operators

**Randomization unit = SEED CLUSTER.** The 4 inferential orders reuse each
seed (same substrate init, same stream content), so (order, seed) cells
sharing a seed are correlated; the exchangeable unit under the null is the
seed. Every test below aggregates within seed first, then sign-flips seeds
with EXACT enumeration (2^8 = 256 flips at N=8; 2^6 = 64 at N=6; min
attainable one-sided p = 1/256 resp. 1/64, both < alpha). This matches the
commissioning precedent (R-015 gates were per-order tests OVER SEEDS).

Unless named otherwise, all A1 operators below use the primary
FULL-representation readout after gate (k)'s common-target or
competence-comparability rule. Cell statistic:
cell(o, s) = median over the 5 tasks of the cell's s*.
Seed aggregate: bar(x)_s = mean over the 4 inferential orders of cell(o, s).
**Test statistic (all operators): the MEAN over seeds of the seed
aggregate.** Exact enumeration of all 2^N sign assignments applied to the
(shifted) seed aggregates; one-sided p = proportion of assignments whose
mean >= the observed mean. **Exactness assumption (stated):** the test is
exact under the null that the seed aggregates are independent across seeds
(disjoint inits and stream content by construction) and SIGN-SYMMETRIC
about the tested point (0, or the shift point ±eps) — the standard
sign-flip exchangeability condition.

- **SUP(A > B) [MEANINGFUL superiority — shifted null]:** one-sided exact
  seed sign-flip on bar(Δ)_s − eps, where
  bar(Δ)_s = mean_o[cell_A(o,s) − cell_B(o,s)], H1: mean > 0 (i.e. Δ > eps),
  alpha = 0.05. Since eps is the smallest meaningful effect, superiority is
  claimed only BEYOND the band; SUP and EQ are mutually exclusive by
  construction. An effect distinguishable from zero but inside ±eps reads as
  practically equivalent (recorded with its unshifted test as descriptive).
  Per-order consistency recorded (non-gating).
- **A1POS(A) [meaningful positive savings — same shift, same rationale]:**
  one-sided exact seed sign-flip on bar(s*_12)_s − eps of arm A, H1: mean > 0,
  alpha = 0.05. This prevents a relative superiority claim when C itself has
  negative or sub-meaningful savings (for example, −0.2 improving to −0.1, or
  +0.02).
- **A1C(A) [required memory-specificity companion]:** old-task relearning
  versus contemporaneous first learning on the matched novel/remapped task,
  under the shared no-boundary-signal schedule in §4. The final statistic,
  common target, meaningful margin, aggregation, and known-answer operating
  characteristics are **TBD BY COMMISSIONING** under gate (k). A1C(C) is a
  required conjunct of an affirmative C reading. This unresolved operator is
  itself a registration blocker; v10 does not authorize substituting a
  favorable statistic after data.
- **EQ(A, B):** TOST — two one-sided exact seed sign-flip tests of bar(Δ)_s
  against +eps and −eps, each at alpha = 0.05 (declared only if BOTH reject).
  **Draft eps = 0.07 s*-units, proposed as the smallest MEANINGFUL
  crossing-time effect:** s* = 0.07 corresponds to relearning
  reaching bar in t2/t1 = (1−0.07)/(1+0.07) ≈ 0.87 of first-exposure time —
  a 13% speed-up. The commissioned known-positive (SGD/kNN, R-015) sits at
  s* = 0.176 ≈ 30% speed-up; we set the smallest arm-difference that could
  matter for the selector claim at ~40% of the known-positive effect.
  Prior commissioning motivates the SCALE; the final registration must freeze
  or replace eps before treatment data. eps is an effect-meaningfulness margin,
  NOT a bias floor and NOT derived from noise (absolute known-zero bias does
  not calibrate a paired difference — shared bias cancels in Δ).
- **INDET(A, B):** neither SUP (either direction) nor EQ → indeterminate;
  recorded; blocks every affirmative reading that cites the contrast.
- **GROW(A) [SECONDARY, NON-GATING — demoted per sol pass 3]:** one-sided
  exact seed sign-flip on bar(g)_s = mean_o[cell s*_13 − cell s*_12] > 0,
  alpha = 0.05. The slope channel has NO commissioned known-positive (SGD's

## 8. What this trial does NOT establish

- The TOTAL effect of contradiction-triggered selection: the estimand is the
  victim criterion CONDITIONAL on a C-generated schedule (§1); the
  timing/rate component is held fixed by the yoke and waits on the
  autonomous-timing follow-up (§2.1).
- A positive C result means only superiority on the eventually registered
  full-representation decodable-savings/readout construct. It does not
  establish endogenous use, knowledge, rent, causal control of action,
  goal genesis, scalar absence, or AGI.
- No mechanism-vs-backprop comparison is made: no parametric treatment arm
  runs here.
- C-null ≠ selectionism-as-such falsified (strong-selection convergence
  caveat; R/U carry selection too — the contrast is about the CULL CRITERION).
- C's lack of a persistent total ranking or fixed maximand is a design
  property to audit, not evidence that no implicit scalar exists. The U arm
  and frozen named surrogates test specific alternatives only.
- Exact-channel known controls certify only the frozen pipeline on which they
  run.
- SUP(U > C) would not vindicate reward-is-enough globally — one regime, one
  scale.
- U-yoked tests Sutton's cull CRITERION at matched schedule, not his full
  mechanism (native replacement-rate ρ requires a separate follow-up).
- The C1 leg is absolute-only (per-arm C1GATE); NO between-arm C1 magnitude
  or selector-causation claim follows from it.
- The slope channel establishes nothing in either direction (uncommissioned:
  no known-positive exists; GROW is annotation-only).
- Full-representation and subset readouts are external decodability views.
  Neither shows that the agent used the decoded information.
- Passive A3 traces do not constitute A3 evidence; full A2 is a separate
  registration.
- The confirmatory scope is fresh samples from the disclosed task family. It
  does not establish generalization to task families held out from
  development.
